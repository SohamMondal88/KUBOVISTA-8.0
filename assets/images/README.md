# KuboVistas image library

These folders are the stable upload locations used by the website. Keep filenames lowercase, descriptive, and route-safe. Prefer AVIF or WebP for photographs, provide a JPEG fallback where a page still requires it, and record the photographer, source, licence, and consent in your content records.

- `destinations/`: destination hero, card, gallery, map, and seasonal images.
- `packages/`: package hero, itinerary, stay, activity, and inclusion images.
- `about/`: verified founder portraits and the KuboVistas origin-story image.
- `logos/`: approved brand marks and wordmarks.
- `favicons/`: browser, pinned-tab, and home-screen icons.
- `general/`: shared editorial, company, CTA, and utility images.

Recommended founder filenames are `about/soham-mondal.webp`, `about/soumyadip-tarafdar.webp`, and `about/founders-story.webp`. Update `company.js` only after the files and their usage rights are verified. Do not upload identity documents or private source files here.
